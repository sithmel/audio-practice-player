audio-practice-player demo

This demo uses sample.mp3 from the same docs/ folder. Replace it or update the media paths below to try a different MP3. The player enhances MP3 <audio> and <video> elements by replacing native controls with practice controls (play/pause ▶/⏸ and repeat 🔃 on the left; speed ⏱ and volume 🔊 popover sliders on the right; full-track scrubber with A/B range markers labeled in parentheses; time shown as current on the left and (ending marker) plus total on the right).
